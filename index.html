MathRush

Welcome to MathRush! Run, solve, and challenge your mathematical abilities. Complete levels, earn points, and become a MathRush champion.

HOW TO PLAY

🏃 Movement & Controls

  • Left Arrow: Switch to the left lane
  • Right Arrow: Switch to the right lane
  • Up Arrow: Jump (for obstacle-dodging feel)

✏️ Solving Math Questions

  • Every few seconds, a math question popup appears with 3 options.
  • Press **1, 2, or 3** on your keyboard, or **click/tap** the answers.
  • Solve questions correctly to keep your runner's speed high.
  • Wrong answers cause you to slow down temporarily and lose a life.

🏆 Scoring & Levels

  • Correct Answer: +10 points (Level 3 awards +20 points!)
  • Fast Answer (under 3s): +5 points speed bonus
  • Level Completion: +50 points bonus
  • Answer 10 questions correctly to complete a level.
  • You have 3 attempts (lives) per level to complete it.
